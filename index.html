From browsing to a better shortlist

A Wheebuy shopping guide with practical checkpoints.

A useful shortlist contains enough information to make a decision. Keep the exact product URL, selected variant, measurements and cost notes together.

When reviewing Wheebuy product links, confirm that the original listing is accessible and that your intended purchasing service currently supports it.

Find a category on CAFANS ↗

  1. Choose the category before the product.

    Begin with shoes, layers or accessories. A focused category makes it easier to compare similar items instead of mixing unrelated prices and specifications.

  2. Record the exact option you want.

    Save the original product link alongside the color, size and variant. Check that the selected option matches the photographs and description.

  3. Compare measurements and available evidence.

    Read the size chart, material information and detailed photos. Where inspection photos are available, review the actual item rather than relying only on promotional images.

  4. Work out the total before ordering.

    Separate the item price from delivery, service charges, currency conversion and applicable import costs. Check current returns and purchase restrictions before paying.

Clear answers before you browse

A few useful questions.

Understand what this directory provides and what still needs checking on the product or purchasing platform.

What is a Wheebuy spreadsheet?

In this guide, a Wheebuy spreadsheet means an organized product shortlist for comparing categories, product links, sizes and costs. This page is an independent guide linking to CAFANS categories, rather than a downloadable spreadsheet or an official Wheebuy service.

Does this page confirm that products work with Wheebuy?

No. A category link does not establish agent compatibility. Check the original product URL, current platform support and purchase restrictions before placing an order.

How should I compare clothing and shoe sizes?

For clothing, compare the seller's garment measurements with a similar item you own. For shoes, check the product's size chart and stated measurement method. Do not assume that the same size label means the same fit across products.

Are shipping, taxes and fees included in a displayed product price?

Do not assume they are included. Check the item price, domestic delivery, service fees, international shipping, currency conversion and any applicable import charges separately.

Are the brand illustrations proof of inventory or authenticity?

No. The illustrations are editorial category artwork. They do not document actual inventory, verify authenticity or imply endorsement by Nike, Louis Vuitton, Gucci or Hermès.
